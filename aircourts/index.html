Investment

Booking platform for sports

Company

Founded in 2015, AirCourts is the leading sports reservation and club management platform in Portugal, serving more than 400 clubs and connecting more than 500k registered players. For sports facilities, AirCourts is a club management software to improve bookings, streamline operations and bring new customers through the door. For players, AirCourts is a marketplace App to easily find and book a court. AirCourts secured angel funding in 2016 and achieved profitability in September 2017.

Role and Investment

Founded the business as CEO and invested in incorporation round.
