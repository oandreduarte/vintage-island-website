How I invest

I invest in startups within my network, prioritising founders I know and trust. I have experience in building SaaS and marketplace companies, so I offer practical experience to help founders to navigate challenges and drive growth.

I usually write €5k - €15k checks, with a few rare exceptions. I put money and get shares. I might also get shares from advisory agreements. These are situations where I help the founders (time and advice) on the basis of a formal agreement, meaning conditions apply to these shares, including vesting or early termination of the agreement.
