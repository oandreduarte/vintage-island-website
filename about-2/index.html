About me

Hi, I'm Andre Duarte

I’m the co-founder and CEO of Carbob and an active angel investor. Previously, I was the founder and CEO of AirCourts, which was later acquired by Playtomic. Before joining tech, I spent a while working in the energy industry at EDP. I hold an MS in Computer Science from the University of Porto (FEUP).

How I invest

Experience from the founder seat.

I invest in startups within my network, prioritising founders I know and trust. I have experience in building SaaS and marketplace companies, so I offer practical experience to help founders navigate challenges (build, scale, exit) and drive growth.

All investments are made via my micro investment company — Vintage Island.

I usually write €5k–€10k checks, with a few rare exceptions. I put money and get shares. Advisory agreements may also include shares, subject to vesting or early termination conditions.
